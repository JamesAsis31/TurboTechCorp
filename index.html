Outage Sequence

How an outage team gets to your site

A repeatable sequence, run the same way whether it's a two-day minor intervention or a full turnaround.

  1. 01

    Contact & scope the call

    Reach the 24/7 hotline or send the outage details — asset type, symptoms, target dates. We confirm scope within hours, not days.

  2. 02

    Mobilize crew & tooling

    We size the crew to the job, pull OEM-aligned tooling, and lock mobilization from our Manila or Doha base.

  3. 03

    Execute on site

    Mechanical discipline on the floor — repair, overhaul or in-situ balancing — integrated with your site safety and shift plan.

  4. 04

    Restart & handover

    Asset restarted, readings verified, documentation handed over. Your rotating equipment, back online and accounted for.
